Discover DHIU

Education with a wider purpose.

Founded in 1986 in Malappuram, Kerala, Darul Huda grew from an Islamic academy into an institution with national reach and international academic connections.

⌘

Our mission

To develop an Islamic educational framework that prepares scholars to learn, practise and share Islam responsibly, while engaging with modern education and the needs of society.

The DHIU approach

Learning that shapes life.

Three foundational values help connect classroom learning with character, practice and service to the community.

01 / LEARN

Ta’leem

Learning through study, discussion, research, debate and a deep engagement with knowledge.

02 / PRACTISE

Tarbiyah

Developing character by putting learning into practice and building thoughtful habits and responsibility.

03 / SERVE

Da’wah

Sharing knowledge with wisdom and contributing positively to people and society.

Four decades of learning

Rooted in tradition. Looking to the future.

Darul Huda was founded in 1986 in Malappuram, Kerala, first as an Islamic academy with the aim of preparing a new generation of Muslim scholars for the responsibilities of education and community service. Its central campus is at Hidaya Nagar, Chemmad.
